### TROUBLESHOOTING

**"Still not working after 5 minutes?"**

1. Check Render logs for errors
2. Hard refresh browser (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try incognito/private window

**"Build failed on Render?"**

Check the Render logs (red text at bottom) and take a screenshot. Common issues:
- Missing file (didn't upload something)
- Wrong folder structure (file not in right path)
- Typo in filename
