Business Settings

Checkout Rules

Applied on every quote page and at checkout. Changes apply to quotes the next time the customer opens them.
Sales tax is charged on the order total plus the rush fee. Rush is an optional checkbox for the customer.
Below the deposit amount, customers pay in full up front. At or above it, they can choose full payment or the deposit, with the balance collected before pickup or shipping. Orders of 1,000+ pieces skip online checkout and come to you for review.

Payment Provider

Shopify is preferred. Without live credentials, checkout automatically falls back to a clearly-labeled mock so the funnel stays testable.
Shopify retired the old single-token setup in Jan 2026 — see the README's "Shopify setup" section for exactly where to find these two values in Shopify's Dev Dashboard.

S&S Activewear

Pulls your blank costs, photos, colors, sizes and stock from S&S. Get your account number and API key from ssactivewear.com under My Account (or email api@ssactivewear.com).
For security the saved key is never shown again. Leave this box blank to keep the saved key.
Each linked garment is priced as: reference garment's tier price + (its S&S cost minus the reference garment's S&S cost) x (1 + markup). Bigger sizes get (size cost minus base cost) x (1 + markup). Example at 60%: a hoodie costing $7.50 more than the tee is +$12.00.

Email Provider

Mock mode logs quote emails below instead of sending real mail — useful for testing the funnel. Choose Gmail to send real emails through your own Gmail account.
This is NOT your regular Gmail password. In your Google Account, turn on 2-Step Verification, then go to Security > App Passwords, create one for "Mail", and paste the 16-character code here.
